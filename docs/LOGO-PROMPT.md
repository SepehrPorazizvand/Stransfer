# Stransfer logo prompt

Direction: high speed plus continuity after interruption. Avoid the earlier S-and-arrow approach. This is a design brief, not a claim of unlimited physical speed or guaranteed hardware recovery. The current executable still uses a temporary icon until a final design is selected.

```text
Create a distinctive, premium app logo for “Stransfer”, a high-speed file-transfer utility that resumes interrupted transfers from saved progress.

Explore a bold abstract symbol inspired by a streamlined capsule in motion. Use two compact, precisely aligned segments separated by a small intentional gap, with the forward segment continuing the same trajectory. The silhouette should communicate rapid movement; the alignment across the gap should suggest continuity after interruption. Integrate one subtle checkpoint detail through negative space.

Avoid letter S monograms, arrows, play/pause buttons, circular reload symbols, folders, and USB icons. Do not combine several familiar icons. Create one memorable silhouette with confident proportions and minimal detail.

Style: contemporary Swiss graphic design, flat vector geometry, slightly asymmetric, strong negative space, legible at 16×16 pixels. Explore electric cyan on near-black, and ensure the design also works in one color. No gradients, shadows, 3D, speed-line clutter, or mockups.

Show three genuinely different silhouette concepts on a plain background. Keep the wordmark “Stransfer” separate from the symbol. For the selected concept, export an icon-only SVG and a transparent PNG, plus a separate wordmark version.
```
